1. Pendahuluan

Selamat datang di GPS Map Camera ("kami", "aplikasi"). Kebijakan Privasi ini dibuat untuk menjelaskan bagaimana aplikasi mengelola, memproses, dan melindungi informasi Anda saat menggunakan aplikasi kamera dokumentasi berbasis GPS ini pada perangkat Android.

Aplikasi ini dirancang khusus untuk para profesional lapangan, surveyor, teknisi konstruksi, petugas inspeksi, dan masyarakat umum yang membutuhkan foto serta video dengan cap stempel data geografis (koordinat, alamat, waktu, kompas, dan cuaca) yang akurat. Kami sangat memprioritaskan kerahasiaan data dan kedaulatan informasi Anda.

2. Izin Perangkat & Data yang Digunakan

Agar fitur stempel geografis dapat berjalan dengan presisi, aplikasi meminta izin akses perangkat keras berikut:

📍 Lokasi Presisi & Perkiraan (GPS) ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Tujuan: Mengukur titik koordinat (Lintang, Bujur, Ketinggian/Elevasi, dan Akurasi) untuk dicetak secara langsung pada stempel watermark foto dan video.
Cakupan: Lokasi hanya diakses secara transien saat Anda membuka layar kamera atau fitur peta manual. Aplikasi tidak melacak di latar belakang (background) ketika aplikasi diminimalkan atau ditutup.
📷 Kamera CAMERA
Tujuan: Menyediakan jendela bidik (viewfinder) kamera, memindai barcode/QR code langsung di perangkat (on-device ML Kit), mengambil foto beresolusi tinggi, serta merekam video dokumentasi lapangan.
🎙️ Mikrofon (Perekam Audio) RECORD_AUDIO
Tujuan: Merekam suara saat Anda memilih mode perekaman video dokumentasi. Audio disimpan bersama file video di penyimpanan lokal Anda dan tidak pernah dikirim ke pihak luar.
💾 Penyimpanan & Galeri Media READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Tujuan: Menyimpan hasil foto dan video ke galeri perangkat Anda (folder publik DCIM/CameraGPS), serta memungkinkan Anda memilih foto dari galeri saat membuat laporan kerja PDF atau stempel ulang.
🧭 Sensor Perangkat (Kompas & Giroskop/Akselerometer) Hardware Sensor
Tujuan: Menghitung arah mata angin (azimuth kompas) serta sudut kemiringan (pitch/roll level) secara langsung di RAM ponsel untuk dicantumkan pada label stempel. Data sensor tidak pernah direkam atau dikirim ke internet.
🌐 Koneksi Internet & Jaringan INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Tujuan: Digunakan terbatas untuk 3 layanan pembantu yang ramah privasi:
  1. Reverse Geocoding (OpenStreetMap Nominatim): Menerjemahkan angka koordinat GPS menjadi nama jalan, kelurahan, kecamatan, dan kota.
  2. Prakiraan Cuaca (Open-Meteo API): Mengambil informasi suhu dan kondisi cuaca setempat berdasarkan koordinat.
  3. Ubin Peta Mini (CartoDB / OpenStreetMap / ESRI): Mengunduh thumbnail peta kecil yang tersemat pada sudut stempel foto.
Catatan: Hanya angka koordinat geografis yang dikirimkan dalam permintaan HTTP ini; tidak ada nama pengguna, identitas perangkat (IMEI), nomor telepon, atau foto yang dikirimkan.

1. Introduction

Welcome to GPS Map Camera ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how the App handles, processes, and protects your information on Android devices.

The App is built specifically for surveyors, civil engineers, field technicians, inspectors, and general users who require verified photographic and video documentation stamped with exact GPS coordinates, address, timestamp, compass azimuth, and weather conditions.

2. Permissions and Information We Collect

To deliver accurate metadata stamping and camera functionality, the App requests permission to access the following hardware and software capabilities:

📍 Precise & Approximate Location ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
Purpose: Calculates real-time geographical coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, Altitude, UTM/MGRS coordinates, and accuracy) to embed directly into photos and videos as watermarks.
Scope: Location data is accessed only while actively viewing the camera preview or manual map screen. The App never runs location services in the background when the app is closed.
📷 Camera CAMERA
Purpose: Displays live camera viewfinder, captures high-resolution photographs, records video clips, and performs on-device Barcode/QR scanning using Google ML Kit.
🎙️ Microphone (Audio Recording) RECORD_AUDIO
Purpose: Records audio synchronized with video clips when video mode is initiated by the user. Audio is saved locally within the user's video file.
💾 Storage & Media Gallery READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
Purpose: Saves stamped photos and recorded videos to your device's media library under DCIM/CameraGPS, and allows selecting photos when generating PDF work reports.
🧭 Hardware Sensors (Compass, Magnetometer & Accelerometer) Hardware Sensor
Purpose: Computes magnetic compass heading (azimuth) and device tilt angle (pitch and roll) directly in memory to render directional badges on photos. Sensor readings are never transmitted online.
🌐 Internet & Network State INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Purpose: Strictly utilized for three privacy-respecting helper services:
  1. Reverse Geocoding (OpenStreetMap Nominatim): Resolves GPS coordinates into street names, districts, and cities.
  2. Live Weather (Open-Meteo API): Retrieves current temperature and atmospheric conditions based on coordinates.
  3. Map Tile Snapshots (CartoDB / OpenStreetMap / ESRI): Downloads small map tiles displayed on the stamp overlay.
Note: Only numerical latitude/longitude coordinates are sent in these requests. No personal identifiers, phone identifiers, or photos are ever transmitted.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all photos, videos, and generated PDF reports reside strictly in your device storage:

  • Full User Ownership: You have complete authority to view, copy, share, or delete any media file at any time using your phone's native Gallery or File Manager.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the App removes local configuration preferences (such as custom templates and notes). Stamped photos saved to your public gallery (`DCIM/CameraGPS`) will remain intact on your phone unless you manually delete them.
  • No Cloud Retention: Since we do not host user data on cloud servers, no account or remote data deletion request is required.
