Izin Perangkat Sensitif yang Digunakan

Aplikasi ini membutuhkan beberapa izin perangkat agar fitur utama pelacakan AR dapat berfungsi secara optimal:

Izin Android Tujuan Penggunaan Pemrosesan Data
CAMERA Menampilkan preview lingkungan nyata secara langsung di layar agar pengguna dapat menjiplak garis sketsa anime di atas kertas fisik. 100% Lokal (Real-time)
FLASHLIGHT Menyalakan lampu senter perangkat untuk menerangi permukaan kertas gambar saat menggambar dalam kondisi minim cahaya. Tanpa Data
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Memungkinkan pengguna memilih foto/gambar anime dari galeri ponsel untuk diubah menjadi panduan garis sketsa (stencil). Hanya File Terpilih

Pernyataan Kepatuhan Kamera: Kami TIDAK melakukan perekaman rahasia, penangkapan foto latar belakang, pengenalan wajah biometrik, ataupun transmisi video ke server manapun di internet. Pemrosesan kamera murni terjadi di memori sementara perangkat dan langsung dibersihkan saat keluar dari studio gambar.

Introduction

Welcome to Draw Anime: AR Trace & Sketch ("the Application"), developed by Nadirah Labs ("we", "us", or "our"). We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding user data in strict adherence to Google Play Developer Policies and global data protection guidelines.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Application operates, what device permissions are required, and how we keep your information private and secure while you enjoy our Augmented Reality drawing tools.

2

Sensitive Device Permissions

The Application requests the following permissions strictly to power its core drawing and tracing features:

Android Permission Purpose Data Handling
CAMERA Provides a live augmented reality viewfinder so you can trace virtual anime stencil outlines onto real paper. 100% Local (Real-time)
FLASHLIGHT Toggles your phone's LED flashlight to illuminate paper while drawing in dim environments. No Data Collected
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Allows you to voluntarily pick photos from your photo gallery to convert into sketch stencils, or save completed artwork. Only Selected Files

Camera Privacy Guarantee: We do NOT record, store, capture background imagery, perform biometric facial analysis, or transmit any video feed across the internet. Camera processing runs entirely in temporary local volatile memory and is wiped immediately upon exiting the drawing session.
